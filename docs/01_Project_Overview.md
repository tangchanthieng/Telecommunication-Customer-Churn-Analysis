### 1. Project Overview

`Business Problem`

Customer churn is one of the most significant challenges facing telecommunication companies. As customer acquisition costs continue to increase, retaining existing customers has become a strategic priority. Without understanding why customers leave, organizations risk revenue loss, declining customer lifetime value, and ineffective marketing investments.

This project applies data analytics and machine learning techniques to identify customers with a high probability of churn and uncover the key business factors influencing customer retention.

---

### 2. Business Background

The telecommunications industry operates in a highly competitive environment where customers can easily switch providers due to:

- Competitive pricing
- Better service packages
- Improved network coverage
- Poor customer support
- Flexible contract options

Traditional retention strategies often react after customers have decided to leave. This project demonstrates how predictive analytics can enable proactive intervention before churn occurs.

---

### 3. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze customer demographics, service usage, and billing behavior.
- Identify the major drivers contributing to customer churn.
- Develop predictive machine learning models to estimate churn probability.
- Evaluate model performance using industry-standard classification metrics.
- Generate actionable business insights for customer retention.
- Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

### 4. Expected Business Outcomes

The project supports business decision-making by enabling organizations to:

- Reduce customer churn
- Improve customer lifetime value (CLTV)
- Increase retention campaign effectiveness
- Prioritize high-risk customers
- Optimize marketing and customer support strategies

---

### 5. Dataset Overview

The dataset contains customer information collected from a telecommunications provider.

Major data categories include:

- Customer demographics
- Geographic information
- Service subscriptions
- Billing information
- Contract details
- Internet usage
- Customer satisfaction
- Churn indicators

Target Variable:

- **Churn Value**
    - 1 = Customer Churned
    - 0 = Customer Retained
